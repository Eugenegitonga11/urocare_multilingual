URO-CARE website — Somali translation review
=============================================

Thank you for checking the Somali text.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder (keep the images in the same folder as the pages).
2. Double-click index.html — this is the Somali homepage.
   From there, use the menu to move between pages, just like the real website.
   Being online lets the fonts and a few photos load.

PAGES TO CHECK
- index.html — Homepage, plus the top menu and footer (these appear on every
  page). A pop-up about ESWL appears after a few seconds; please check it too.
- urology-services.html — Services page (menu: Adeegyada). Click the unit tabs
  (Burooyinka, Prostate-ka, ...) and the FAQ category buttons to see every card
  and question; click each question to open its answer.
- book-appointment.html — Booking page (button: Qabso Ballan). Fill in the form
  with test details and click through all 3 steps to see every label, error
  message, dropdown option and the summary. The last button opens WhatsApp —
  you do not need to send the message.

HOW TO SEND CORRECTIONS
The easiest way: a screenshot of the wrong text with the correct Somali
written next to it, or a message in the form:
   Page / section:  ...
   Current text:    ...
   Correct text:    ...

Medical terms in brackets (e.g. "BPH", "ESWL", "Penile Implant", "Nephrectomy")
were kept in English on purpose because patients often know them that way.
Please tell us if a Somali term would be clearer.

Links to other pages (About, ESWL, Laboratory, Pharmacy, Blog) still open the
English website — those pages will be translated next.
